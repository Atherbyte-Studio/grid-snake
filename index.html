      Settings

      Edges

      Changing this restarts the current run. Best scores are kept separately for each mode and edge setting. Your game mode is chosen on the home screen.

      Sound

      Short sound effects for eating, pick-ups, level-ups and game over. They start after your first tap or key press.

      Golden apples

      When on, a golden apple appears after every 10 new points. It stays for 5 seconds, then disappears. Eating it adds 5 golden segments to your snake and 5 points; those 5 points do not count towards the next apple. Changing this restarts the current run, and best scores are kept separately for golden games.
