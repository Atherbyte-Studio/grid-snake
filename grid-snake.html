      How a duel works
      • Win two rounds. A round lasts 90 seconds. Crash and the round goes to your rival.
      • Food is +1. When the clock runs out, the higher score wins. A tie goes to 15 seconds of sudden death: the first point wins.
      • Crashes: walls, your own body and your rival's body. Both heads into the same cell, or swapping cells, crashes both: then the higher score takes the round, equal scores are a draw.
      • Draws give nobody a point and a fresh round is played. After three draws the match is decided on round wins, then total points.
      • Events: each round brings one: Vanishing Grid, Portal Shift, Laser Sweep, Moving Walls or The Core (+3). Danger is always shown before it can hurt, and it only hurts a head that runs into it.
      • A fair rival: the same speed, the same rules, and only what you can see on screen.

        Settings

        Difficulty

        Easy starts slow and tops out at a calmer speed, Normal sits in between, and Hard is the original fast game. Phones start on Easy. Changing this restarts the current run. Scores count the same on every difficulty.

        Touch controls

        Turn buttons: two big buttons, left and right of the way the snake is heading. Arrows: four fixed directions. Swipe only: no buttons, swipe anywhere. Swiping always works. Touch screens only.

        Edges

        Changing this restarts the current run. Best scores are kept separately for each mode and edge setting. Your game mode is chosen on the home screen.

        Sound

        Short sound effects for eating, pick-ups, level-ups and game over. They start after your first tap or key press.

        Volume

        100%

        Vibration

        A short buzz when you crash or get caught. Only on devices that support it.

        Animations

        Auto follows your device's "reduce motion" setting. Off removes slides, bursts, shaking and the crash animation.

        Golden apples

        When on, a golden apple appears after every 10 new points. It stays for 5 seconds, then disappears. Eating it adds 5 golden segments to your snake and 5 points; those 5 points do not count towards the next apple. It does not apply to Apple Run. Changing this restarts the current run, and best scores are kept separately for golden games.

        Help and about

        How to play

        Classic. Eat food, grow, and do not hit walls or yourself. Wrap mode lets you pass through the edges.

        Time Attack. Score as much as you can before the clock runs out.

        Survival. It gets harder as time passes, with obstacles and pick-ups to help.

        Apple Run. You are the apple. Outrun the snake, use Dash, and drop banana peels and decoys to make it slip.

        About and privacy

        Grid Snake 1.1.1. Made with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No ads, no accounts, no tracking.

        Your scores, achievements and settings are stored only on this device, in your browser's local storage. Nothing is sent anywhere.
